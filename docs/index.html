ABOUT Community First AFC

Community First AFC is a multicultural social service agency that provides adult foster care services to older adults and individuals with disabilities 16 years or older in Massachusetts. Our organization operates from the basis of this simple phrase “We CARE”, demonstrating our mission and commitment to ensuring that we utilize a person-centered approach in working with the individuals we are privileged to serve.

The dedicated team of Case Managers and RNs at Community First are equipped with over 25 years of experience in the adult foster care industry as well experience working for various sectors in the medical and human services fields. We are understanding, culturally sensitive and empathetic to the needs of each member and our goal is to provide optimal service to the families we serve.

Our Mission
We strive to create a culturally inclusive environment that focuses on the holistic development and care of our members, employees, and stakeholders.

Our Vision
To support positive community connections that ultimately transform and improve the quality of life for the employees and members served in their preferred environment.

Our Commitment to You and Your Family

Communicate

Communication is a critical part in the provision and demonstration of care to our members. We maintain good communication with each member, their caregiver, and all other members of their multi-disciplinary team (MDT) to develop a comprehensive and individualized care Plan, specially tailored to meet the needs of the individual which will seek to enrich their lives. It is important that communication is maintained to ensure Care plans remain current and effective.

Advocate

Our team possesses strong advocacy skills and do so on behalf of every individual we serve to ensure they have access to all necessary benefits, services, and resources they need in enhancing their health, safety, wellbeing, and the opportunity to live a meaningful life.

Refer

We continuously assess and evaluate our members to ensure they are being referred for services for which they may be eligible as soon as the need arises. Service providers such as - therapists, adult day programs, Department of Developmental Services (DDS), Department of Transitional Assistance (DTA), Mass Housing, Fuel Assistance, Social Security and an array of other external collaterals.

Educate

We ensure the member and caregiver are thoroughly informed and educated about each recommended service, the reason for the referral, and obtain at least a verbal consent/assent from the caregiver and/or member.
